## LinkedIn

Counterpoint is a co-facilitator for brainstorming meetings, built for
[EVENT].

It listens to a Google Meet call with consent, and instead of interrupting
whenever it has an idea, it waits for a real conversational opening — a
genuine pause, not just a gap between words. When one arrives, it can offer a
short critique, an alternative, or a quick piece of outside evidence. If the
group moves on before an opening ever comes, the thought is discarded, not
saved for later or blurted out late.

The turn-taking logic — when a suggestion is allowed to speak, held, or
dropped — is the core of the project and is unit-tested as a deterministic
state machine, independent of audio, Chrome, or any LLM.

Built with OpenAI Realtime for the voice session and Exa for on-demand
external evidence.
